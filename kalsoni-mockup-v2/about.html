Our story

Kalsoni began with a question.

What if more women could participate when performance apparel was designed with them in mind?

That question grew from years on the court, coaching young athletes, and contributing to community-led research exploring how culturally appropriate athleticwear could remove barriers to movement.

What began as research became something bigger.

It became Kalsoni.

What guides us

What we believe

Movement can transform lives.

It supports our health, builds confidence, and brings people together. Every woman should be able to experience those benefits while feeling fully like herself — strong, supported, and welcome.

Designed with intention

Designed for modesty. Built for performance.

We put women first, beginning with how she moves and what she needs to feel comfortable, covered, and ready. Every silhouette, pocket, fabric, and layer is considered for sport, training, the outdoors, and everyday movement.

Fabric
Breathable, flexible performance materials selected for comfort, coverage, and repeat wear.
Function
Secure sport hijabs, purposeful pockets, thumbholes, side zips, and movement-first construction.
Fit
Faith-inclusive silhouettes designed to move away from the body, offered from XS–5XL.
